# Voiceprint — Josh Fryszer
_Computed 2026-09-08 from 6 recorded calls (317 turns, 11,022 words), contrasted against 59 sentinel posts._

**The corpus is speech, not writing.** Every number below is how Josh talks. Spoken
sentences run shorter and hedge more than written ones for everybody, so the rhythm
figures are a baseline for his thinking, not a target for his posts. The lexicon travels
between the two registers; the sentence lengths do not.

## Rhythm
- Sentences: mean **10.2** words (median 5, sd 16.3); 62% short (<8w), 14% long (>18w).
- ~14 words per turn (median).
## Register
- I-vs-we: **6.46** (>1 = speaks as himself, <1 = speaks for a company).
- Contractions 59.52/1k · hedging 22.68/1k · 'you'-address 32.03/1k · turns containing a question 21%.
## Surface habits

- Opening mix: statement 269, question 24, first_person 18, consensus_claim 3, number_claim 3.

  reproduce them as authenticity. The voice guide is explicit that this is the one
