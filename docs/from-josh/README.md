from a recording of Josh talking, not from the post archive.

## What was done to them

**Kept verbatim.** Including the transcription errors, the false starts, the repetitions and the
half-finished sentences. That is the point of the file. Anything tidied is a different voice.

**Speaker labels corrected.** The exports labelled Josh as "Me" and everyone else as "Them" or by
name. Labels are now Josh plus a role.

**Names removed.** Every person, client, client's customer, vendor and internal product name is
replaced with a bracketed descriptor. Josh's own details stay in, because they are his.

## Two cautions before sending

The 27 August call contains a moment where the client names one of their own customers and says in
the same sentence that it cannot be mentioned externally. That name and its location are gone, but
the surrounding figures are not.

The 31 August call keeps its clinical vertical language, because removing it leaves nothing readable.
Anyone who knows that market could infer the client. Josh should decide whether that is acceptable or
whether to send four calls instead of five.
